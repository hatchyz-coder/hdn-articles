LHub is designed to connect clinic-facing workflows around a LINE Official Account, including reservations, questionnaires, payment guidance, customer management, and follow-up messaging.

## Start with the patient journey

Before configuring tools, the clinic should map the steps a patient takes from first contact to reservation, questionnaire completion, payment, and follow-up. The goal is to reduce unnecessary switching between channels.

## Use clear status information

Operational labels and customer-management information can help staff understand whether a patient is waiting for a response, has completed a form, or needs another action. The exact configuration should match the clinic's real workflow.

## Design reservation and questionnaire guidance together

Patients should be able to understand what they need to complete before the visit and what happens next. Short instructions and clear next actions reduce avoidable back-and-forth.

## Connect messages to real workflow events

Greeting messages, automated responses, form completion, payment, and reservation completion can be used as points for delivering the next piece of information. The content should remain simple and should not assume unsupported product capabilities.

## Practical takeaway

The most useful implementation is not the one with the most automation. It is the one that reduces missed handoffs and makes the next action clear for both patients and staff.
