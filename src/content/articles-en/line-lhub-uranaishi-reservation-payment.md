This guide shows independent fortune‑tellers how to design a practical LINE-based workflow—lead capture, booking calendar, prepayment, reminders and post-session follow-up—using LHub or similar tools. It covers the operational checklist, cancellation policy considerations, and compliance points (e.g., Japan’s Act on Specified Commercial Transactions).

Why automate?

Independent fortune‑tellers often spend excessive time on scheduling, payment collection and pre-session intake. Using LINE—the dominant messaging app in Japan—as the customer touchpoint shortens the booking funnel and reduces friction. LINE supports reservation flows and can carry booking links or integrated reservation widgets to complete appointments within the app.([lycorp.co.jp](https://www.lycorp.co.jp/en/company/global/?utm_source=openai))

Core operational flow

1) Landing and routing on LINE Official Account
- Set the profile and rich menu to show “Book a session,” “Price list,” and a link to your legal disclosures (required for commercial offerings). Use LINE’s reservation features or embed a booking widget.([guide.line.me](https://guide.line.me/ja/services/reserve-with-line.html?utm_source=openai))

2) Intake form
- Collect key information at booking: preferred divination method, issue category, brief background. This prepares you for a focused session.

3) Prepayment
- Require payment to confirm the slot (full payment or deposit). Prepayment is effective at reducing no‑shows and payment disputes; many booking systems emphasize this design.([bizly.jp](https://bizly.jp/30-recommended-reservation-systems-comparison/?utm_source=openai))

4) Reminders and cancellation policy
- Send automated reminders (e.g., 24 hours and 1 hour before). Publish your cancellation and refund policy clearly on the booking screen.

5) Post-session follow‑up
- Use tags/segments to deliver tailored follow‑ups and offers; automation tools can send session summaries, next‑session suggestions or special offers. LHub supports these flows as a turnkey option.([hdnjapan.com](https://hdnjapan.com/lhub.html))

Compliance and risk control

- Disclosure: If you sell paid services via LINE or other SNS, ensure you meet disclosure obligations under Japan’s Act on Specified Commercial Transactions (display of business name, contact, prices, refund policy, etc.).([no-trouble.caa.go.jp](https://www.no-trouble.caa.go.jp/qa/advertising.html?utm_source=openai))
- Privacy: Handle sensitive client messages carefully; define retention and access rules.
- Payments: Verify the payment provider’s terms for individual sellers and the refund/dispute processes.

Getting started (practical tip)

Begin with the smallest workable automation: a booking form + prepayment link + 24‑hour reminder. Measure no‑show rates and customer satisfaction for a month, then add segmentation and follow‑up campaigns.
