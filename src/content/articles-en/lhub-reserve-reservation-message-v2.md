This guide examines friction at the 予約完了後 stage and focuses on workflow design before product configuration.

## Start with the moment of friction

The topic is 予約完了後こそ設計する。来店・来院までの空白を埋める案内. At the 予約完了後 stage, teams should first observe where people hesitate instead of starting with software settings. The practical problem is that 予約できた安心感のあとに必要事項を忘れやすい. A useful workflow makes the next action obvious without exposing internal process complexity.

## Design one next action

The core design principle is to 次に必要な準備だけを順番に示す. Extra choices can look helpful but often increase decision load. Move secondary information later and keep the immediate action visible.

## Plan the exception path

A normal path is not enough. When 日程や条件が変更になった場合, define where human judgment starts, who owns the response, and how the customer can leave the automated path without getting stuck.

## Measure operational friction

Watch 確認問い合わせと直前の混乱. Repeated questions, handoffs, and pauses often reveal more than a feature checklist. Improve one recurring friction point at a time.

## Keep product claims separate from workflow design

Confirm product-specific capabilities in current official documentation before implementation. This reserve article deliberately focuses on workflow principles and does not assume an undocumented product function.

## Keep the human work human

Use automation for predictable steps and preserve human attention for exceptions, reassurance, and judgment. A good workflow reduces unnecessary repetition without making the experience feel closed or mechanical.
