PMDA conducts compliance inspections of facilities that manufacture specified processed cells under Japan's regenerative medicine safety framework. The official material describes the application process, facility requirements, and documents used to assess whether a manufacturing site meets structural and operational standards.

## Start with the facility map

Clinics and related operators should be able to explain how rooms, clean areas, storage areas, and movement routes are separated. A clear floor plan makes it easier to understand how contamination and mix-up risks are controlled.

## Keep measurement records usable

Environmental monitoring, equipment checks, and other verification records should be organized so that the date, method, equipment, result, and responsible person can be traced.

## Document utilities and equipment

Water, gases, sterilization equipment, air handling, and other utilities relevant to processing should be described together with the procedures used to maintain and verify them.

## Clarify outsourced work

If part of the process is outsourced, the contract and responsibility boundary should be clear. The organization should know who performs each task, who reviews the result, and how deviations are handled.

## Practical takeaway

Inspection readiness is not about producing more documents at the last minute. It comes from aligning the actual facility, daily records, and written procedures before an inspection is scheduled.
