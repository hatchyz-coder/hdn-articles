# How Lawyers Can Streamline Client Consultations with LINE and LHub

## 1️⃣ Lower the Consultation Barrier

Do not assume an unverified product capability. Define the required information, owner, and next action first, then confirm product-specific functions in official documentation before implementation.
- **Automated pre‑consultation**: Leverage LINE’s auto‑reply to collect basic information before the lawyer engages, ensuring a smoother first meeting.
- **Secure information flow**: Avoid detailed legal advice over LINE; instead, direct clients to the office for the actual discussion.


## 2️⃣ Centralise Appointment & Order Management

- **Integrated booking**: With LHub, appointments are managed directly through LINE, visible in a single dashboard, cutting staff time spent on manual coordination.
- **Automated payments**: Offer multiple payment options—credit card, PayPay, convenience store, or bank transfer—while tracking status in real‑time.
- **Case tracking**: Log consultation history, progress, and fee details in one place, enabling clear oversight when handling multiple cases.

> LINE Official Account pricing: Free communication plan (200 messages/month), Light plan (5,000¥, 5,000 messages), Standard plan (15,000¥, 30,000 messages)【lme.jp】.

## 3️⃣ Maintain Ongoing Engagement

- **Centralised history**: Keep all LINE chats, bookings, and orders in one view, simplifying transition from initial to follow‑up consultations.
- **Automated legal updates**: Use segmented messaging to send updates on legal reforms or case law to relevant clients, reinforcing the lawyer’s expertise.
- **Follow‑ups**: Send reminders for upcoming appointments and offer next‑step advice automatically, keeping clients engaged.

## 4️⃣ Manage Fees and Deposits Effectively

- **Clear fee presentation**: Publish fee structures on the LINE official page to reduce client uncertainty.
- **Real‑time payment tracking**: Sync payment data with the order system to monitor deposits and refunds instantly.
- **Multiple payment options**: Increase client convenience by supporting diverse payment methods.

## 5️⃣ Deliver Legal Updates

- **Timely notifications**: Push important reforms or judgments to clients’ devices via segmented messaging.
- **Showcase expertise**: Regular updates serve as proof of professional knowledge and strengthen trust.

## 6️⃣ Create a Path for Continued Consultations

- **Regular check‑ins**: Send monthly follow‑up messages to confirm client status and propose next steps.
- **Transparent history**: Share past consultation details to ease the flow of subsequent discussions.
- **Feedback loops**: Collect satisfaction data to refine services.

## Takeaway

Combining LINE Official Accounts with LHub allows law firms to manage intake, appointments, and payments in a single platform, while reducing the psychological barrier for clients. When implementing, remember to comply with the lawyer’s statutory advertising rules and ensure robust data security.
