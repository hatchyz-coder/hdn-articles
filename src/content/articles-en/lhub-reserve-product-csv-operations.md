Small recurring tasks quietly consume attention. This guide examines one LHub workflow from the perspective of reducing friction for staff and customers.

## Start with real operational friction

LHub documents CSV-based bulk registration and bulk updating for product-group information (Official information: https://l-hub.info/).

The goal is not to automate everything. Separate repetitive, predictable work from moments that still require judgment. A useful workflow reduces a real interruption for staff or customers.

## Design the workflow before the screen

Write down who starts the task, what information is needed, what should happen next, and what happens when the normal path fails. Keep an escape route for exceptions instead of forcing every case through automation.

## Make convenience visible

Look for repeated explanations, waiting, forgotten follow-ups, unnecessary switching, or work that depends on one person being available. Those are better design signals than adding more functions.

## Keep the human work human

Use only capabilities documented by LHub (https://l-hub.info/). Let automation handle predictable steps and leave judgment, reassurance, and exceptional cases to people.
