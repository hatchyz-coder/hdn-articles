This guide examines friction at the 予約前 stage and focuses on workflow design before product configuration.

## Start with the moment of friction

The topic is 予約前FAQは増やしすぎない。迷いを減らす質問設計. At the 予約前 stage, teams should first observe where people hesitate instead of starting with software settings. The practical problem is that 情報が多すぎて自分に必要な回答を探せない. A useful workflow makes the next action obvious without exposing internal process complexity.

## Design one next action

The core design principle is to 予約判断に直結する質問から並べる. Extra choices can look helpful but often increase decision load. Move secondary information later and keep the immediate action visible.

## Plan the exception path

A normal path is not enough. When 個別判断が必要な質問の場合, define where human judgment starts, who owns the response, and how the customer can leave the automated path without getting stuck.

## Measure operational friction

Watch 同じ質問の反復と予約前離脱. Repeated questions, handoffs, and pauses often reveal more than a feature checklist. Improve one recurring friction point at a time.

## Keep product claims separate from workflow design

Confirm product-specific capabilities in current official documentation before implementation. This reserve article deliberately focuses on workflow principles and does not assume an undocumented product function.

## Keep the human work human

Use automation for predictable steps and preserve human attention for exceptions, reassurance, and judgment. A good workflow reduces unnecessary repetition without making the experience feel closed or mechanical.
