LHub (launched 2025) integrates LINE Official Account workflows with booking, contract guidance and payments. This guide outlines practical setup steps, payment and compliance considerations, a 90‑day pilot plan and KPIs to measure impact in rental operations.([metaps-payment.com](https://www.metaps-payment.com/company/newsrelease20250603.html?utm_source=openai))

Lead

In Japan, LINE is often the default customer touchpoint. LHub — formally released in 2025 — aims to unify booking, contract guidance and payments on top of LINE Official Accounts. This article gives practical implementation advice for rental brokers and property managers, focusing on operational design, payment integration and pilot testing.([metaps-payment.com](https://www.metaps-payment.com/company/newsrelease20250603.html?utm_source=openai))

Why LINE matters

Major property portals and many agencies already use LINE official accounts to handle inquiries and viewing reservations, making LINE a practical channel to shorten the path from inquiry to viewing and contract. Government guidance also recognizes that online workflows can streamline web-based viewings and contract steps.([page.line.me](https://page.line.me/263vjium?utm_source=openai))

What LHub enables (practical view)

- Booking and schedule management with automated reminders.([metaps-payment.com](https://www.metaps-payment.com/company/newsrelease20250603.html?utm_source=openai))
- Pre-contract guidance and document delivery via LINE messaging.
- Upfront fee payments via embedded payment flows; recurring rent collection possible through payment gateway integrations (confirm fees and settlement timing).([gmo-pg.com](https://www.gmo-pg.com/service/issue/dx/?utm_source=openai))
- Targeted communications to tenants (maintenance notices, surveys).

Implementation checklist

1) Map customer journey end-to-end (friend add → property view → booking → application → payment).
2) Define reservation rules and cancellation policy.
3) Choose a payment gateway and confirm support for recurring billing and reconciliation processes.([gmo-pg.com](https://www.gmo-pg.com/service/issue/dx/?utm_source=openai))
4) Ensure privacy and consent management for data collected via LINE.
5) Verify electronic contract procedures and necessary identity verification steps in line with legal guidance.([mlit.go.jp](https://www.mlit.go.jp/common/001252314.pdf?utm_source=openai))

Pilot plan (90 days)

- Weeks 0–2: implement flows for 1–3 properties and finalize messaging.
- Weeks 3–6: train staff and simulate common inquiries.
- Weeks 7–12: track KPIs (booking→show rate, show→application rate, application→payment success rate, staff time saved) and iterate.

Risks and mitigations

- Payment failures and refunds — define a clear policy and test refund flows.([gmo-pg.com](https://www.gmo-pg.com/service/issue/dx/?utm_source=openai))
- Data handling — reduce sensitive data in chat and point users to secured forms where possible.
- Legal requirements — align e-contract handling with official guidance and industry association recommendations.([mlit.go.jp](https://www.mlit.go.jp/common/001252314.pdf?utm_source=openai))

Conclusion

LHub presents a viable option for operators seeking to centralize LINE-based reservations, contract guidance and payments. Success depends on careful payment integration, privacy design and staged pilots with KPI-driven iterations.([metaps-payment.com](https://www.metaps-payment.com/company/newsrelease20250603.html?utm_source=openai))
