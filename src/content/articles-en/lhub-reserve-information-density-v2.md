This guide examines friction at the メッセージ受信時 stage and focuses on workflow design before product configuration.

## Start with the moment of friction

The topic is 伝えるほど伝わらない。LINE上の情報量を減らす編集術. At the メッセージ受信時 stage, teams should first observe where people hesitate instead of starting with software settings. The practical problem is that 一通に情報を詰め込みすぎて行動が分からない. A useful workflow makes the next action obvious without exposing internal process complexity.

## Design one next action

The core design principle is to 一通一目的で次の行動を目立たせる. Extra choices can look helpful but often increase decision load. Move secondary information later and keep the immediate action visible.

## Plan the exception path

A normal path is not enough. When 複数の重要事項を同時に伝える場合, define where human judgment starts, who owns the response, and how the customer can leave the automated path without getting stuck.

## Measure operational friction

Watch 読み飛ばしと再確認. Repeated questions, handoffs, and pauses often reveal more than a feature checklist. Improve one recurring friction point at a time.

## Keep product claims separate from workflow design

Confirm product-specific capabilities in current official documentation before implementation. This reserve article deliberately focuses on workflow principles and does not assume an undocumented product function.

## Keep the human work human

Use automation for predictable steps and preserve human attention for exceptions, reassurance, and judgment. A good workflow reduces unnecessary repetition without making the experience feel closed or mechanical.
