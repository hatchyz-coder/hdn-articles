This guide examines friction at the 登録直後 stage and focuses on workflow design before product configuration.

## Start with the moment of friction

The topic is 友だち追加直後に迷わせない。最初の一画面から整えるLINE導線. At the 登録直後 stage, teams should first observe where people hesitate instead of starting with software settings. The practical problem is that 何をすればよいか分からず離脱しやすい. A useful workflow makes the next action obvious without exposing internal process complexity.

## Design one next action

The core design principle is to 次の行動を一つに絞る. Extra choices can look helpful but often increase decision load. Move secondary information later and keep the immediate action visible.

## Plan the exception path

A normal path is not enough. When 問い合わせ内容が想定外だった場合, define where human judgment starts, who owns the response, and how the customer can leave the automated path without getting stuck.

## Measure operational friction

Watch 最初の質問と手戻り. Repeated questions, handoffs, and pauses often reveal more than a feature checklist. Improve one recurring friction point at a time.

## Keep product claims separate from workflow design

Confirm product-specific capabilities in current official documentation before implementation. This reserve article deliberately focuses on workflow principles and does not assume an undocumented product function.

## Keep the human work human

Use automation for predictable steps and preserve human attention for exceptions, reassurance, and judgment. A good workflow reduces unnecessary repetition without making the experience feel closed or mechanical.
