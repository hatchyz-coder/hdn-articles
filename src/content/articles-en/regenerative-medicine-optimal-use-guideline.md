For innovative regenerative medicine products with new mechanisms of action, Japan publishes optimal-use guidelines that describe requirements and considerations for patients and medical institutions.

## Confirm the product-specific guideline

Before introducing a product, the clinic should identify whether an optimal-use guideline applies and confirm the latest version. Product-specific requirements can differ materially.

## Translate the guideline into workflow

Patient selection criteria, pre-treatment testing, administration procedures, monitoring, and follow-up should be mapped into the clinic's actual clinical process rather than left as a reference document.

## Align patient information

Consent and patient-information materials should reflect the risks, monitoring requirements, and limitations described in the relevant guideline and product information.

## Assign responsibilities

The clinic should define who checks eligibility, who confirms required tests, who records administration, and who owns follow-up and safety reporting.

## Practical takeaway

The guideline is most useful when it is converted into a working checklist. The goal is not simply to store the document, but to ensure that the clinic's real workflow remains consistent with current product-specific requirements.
