This guide distinguishes provisional intake from confirmed decisions and keeps clinical judgment with qualified professionals.

## The operational boundary

Explain the next communication and follow-up steps after a consultation without prescribing a clinical outcome. A request being received does not mean the intended result is confirmed.

## Ownership and verification

Define who checks the request, which information is needed, and what remains uncertain. Provide a clear next step without committing to an unverified outcome.

## Human judgment and privacy

Clinical decisions belong with qualified professionals. Do not collect or share more patient information than is necessary for a legitimate purpose.

## Handoff and correction

Record verified facts separately from assumptions. When an error is discovered, identify the responsible owner, correct the communication, and update the relevant instructions.

## Product capability

Do not assume LHub or LINE capabilities that have not been verified against official documentation. Design a workable human process before choosing tools.

## Continuous improvement

Review repeated questions, inconsistent responses, and failed handoffs. Use those observations to refine the process rather than overstating automation benefits.
