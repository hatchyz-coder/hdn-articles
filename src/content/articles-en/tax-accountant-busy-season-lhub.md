# How Tax Accountants Can Navigate Busy Seasons with LINE and LHub

## 1. The Reality of the Busy Season

- **Year‑end adjustment / statutory reports**: Concentrated around January.
- **Individual tax returns**: Peak in February‑March.
- **Corporate accounts**: Peaks in May for March‑ending firms.

The volume of communication across phone, email, and in‑person meetings becomes fragmented, leading to lost follow‑ups and increased time spent searching for information.

## 2. Centralizing Information with LINE Official Account
LINE Official Account provides a platform for businesses to communicate with customers. For tax advisors, the benefits include:

| Feature | Benefit |
|---------|---------|
| Adding friends | Creates a single touchpoint for clients |
| Message delivery | Sends timely updates on deadlines and tax changes |
| Chat | Handles real‑time client questions |

## 3. One‑Stop Solution with LHub

### 3‑1. Booking Management
- Calendar sync ensures interview slots are visible.
- Eliminates the need for phone/email scheduling.

### 3‑2. Order & Payment
- Invoice and payment status updates appear instantly on LINE.
- Reduces payment‑delay risks.

### 3‑3. Customer Management
- Consolidates past questions, contracts, and client data.
- Segment‑based delivery allows tailored communications.

## 4. Practical Implementation During Peak
1. **Pre‑season preparation**: Set client‑specific document deadlines and send reminders via LINE.
2. **Booking automation**: Manage appointments in LHub and display available times.
3. **Integrated payment flow**: Issue invoices and track payments within LINE.
4. **Segmented broadcasts**: Send tax‑change updates to corporate vs. individual clients.

## 5. Expected Benefits
- **Information centralization**: Drastically cuts search time.
- **Operational efficiency**: Automates booking, payment, and data management, freeing staff for core tax work.
- **Client trust**: Real‑time updates and smooth queries increase satisfaction.
- **Peak‑season relief**: Maintains consistent workloads, reducing overtime.

## 6. Adoption Roadmap
1. Open a LINE Official Account.
2. Register for LHub and link the account.
3. Import client lists and set up segments.
4. Configure booking and payment settings.
5. Conduct internal training.

**Key Tips**
- One LINE account manages both official and LHub operations.
- Segment‑based broadcasts avoid confusion.
- LHub’s intuitive UI works for non‑technical staff.

## 7. Conclusion
Integrating LINE Official Account with LHub equips tax advisors to navigate the November‑to‑May peak smoothly. By consolidating inquiries, bookings, and payments, firms can eliminate information gaps, elevate client relationships, and reduce administrative strain—all while maintaining high service quality.
