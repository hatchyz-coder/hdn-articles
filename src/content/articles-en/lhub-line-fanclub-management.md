For artists and small management teams in Japan, using LINE as the primary touchpoint—combined with a tool like LHub—reduces friction across membership fees, ticket sales, merchandise and targeted messaging. This article explains practical setup steps, LINE Mini App ticketing options, and operational checks to run a sustainable fanclub. ([prtimes.jp](https://prtimes.jp/main/html/rd/p/000001600.000129774.html?utm_source=openai))

Lead

Independent artists and small talent teams often juggle ticketing, merch, subscription fees and content delivery with limited staff. Centralizing these flows on LINE and using an operations layer such as LHub can cut manual work and make “who did what” visible. ([prtimes.jp](https://prtimes.jp/main/html/rd/p/000001600.000129774.html?utm_source=openai))

Why LINE?

LINE has a large domestic reach, making it an effective primary channel to reduce friction from discovery to purchase and attendance. Designing the flow—friend registration → payment → ticket issuance → event reminders → post-event follow-up—inside LINE lowers drop-off risk. ([prtimes.jp](https://prtimes.jp/main/html/rd/p/000001600.000129774.html?utm_source=openai))

What LHub brings to the table

- Unified membership management: tie fee payments to individual member records and automate reminders. ([hdnjapan.com](https://hdnjapan.com/lhub.html))
- Ticketing support: configure sale windows, quantity caps, and automatic post-purchase messaging. For seamless ticket purchase-to-entry experiences, combine LINE-based flows with LINE Mini App or ticket APIs. ([developers.line.biz](https://developers.line.biz/ja/docs/line-mini-app/demo/mixwayapi-demo/?utm_source=openai))
- Merch and cart flow: product listing, inventory checks and abandoned-cart notifications help reduce order errors. ([hdnjapan.com](https://hdnjapan.com/lhub.html))
- Segmented messaging: use purchasing and engagement signals to deliver relevant offers and limited-content notices via LINE's audience tools. ([lycbiz.com](https://www.lycbiz.com/jp/manual/OfficialAccountManager/broadcast-demographic/?list=7171&utm_source=openai))

Practical checklist for small teams

1) Map the minimum viable flow (one payment stream first). ([hdnjapan.com](https://hdnjapan.com/lhub.html))
2) Choose ticket issuance: QR-based entry, transfer rules, and re-issue process. Verify technical feasibility with your ticketing partner. ([developers.line.biz](https://developers.line.biz/ja/docs/line-mini-app/demo/mixwayapi-demo/?utm_source=openai))
3) Start with a few segments (e.g., frequent buyers, inactive members, new sign-ups) and expand. ([lycbiz.com](https://www.lycbiz.com/jp/manual/OfficialAccountManager/broadcast-demographic/?list=7171&utm_source=openai))
4) Measure and iterate monthly on friend growth, conversion, entry rate and retention. ([hdnjapan.com](https://hdnjapan.com/lhub.html))

Conclusion

LINE’s wide reach in Japan and the operational capabilities of tools like LHub make it possible for small teams to run professional, data-informed fanclubs without ballooning administrative overhead. Confirm payment partners, ticketing flow, and privacy rules before launch and start small. ([prtimes.jp](https://prtimes.jp/main/html/rd/p/000001600.000129774.html?utm_source=openai))
