Small recurring interactions quietly consume attention. This guide looks at one LHub workflow from the perspective of reducing friction for staff and customers.

## Start with the repeated friction

LHub documents workflows that combine reservations, forms, payments, and customer management around an official LINE account (Official information: https://l-hub.info/).

The practical goal is not to automate every interaction. It is to remove one repeated interruption that staff and customers experience again and again.

## Design the flow before configuring the tool

Write down what happens today, where people wait, where the same explanation is repeated, and which step genuinely requires judgment. Keep only the information needed for the next action.

## Keep exceptions human

A good workflow includes an exit for cases that do not fit the normal path. Automation should handle predictable steps; people should handle judgment, reassurance, and unusual situations.

## Improve from real questions

After launch, watch the questions that still come back. Repeated questions show where the wording, timing, or sequence needs improvement. The goal is not more automation. It is a clearer experience for both staff and customers.

Use only capabilities documented by LHub (https://l-hub.info/) and adjust the workflow to the reality of the operation.
