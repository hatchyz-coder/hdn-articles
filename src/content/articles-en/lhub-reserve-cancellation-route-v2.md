This guide examines friction at the 予定変更 stage and focuses on workflow design before product configuration.

## Start with the moment of friction

The topic is キャンセル導線を隠さない。変更したい時に迷わせない設計. At the 予定変更 stage, teams should first observe where people hesitate instead of starting with software settings. The practical problem is that 変更方法が見つからず連絡が遅れる. A useful workflow makes the next action obvious without exposing internal process complexity.

## Design one next action

The core design principle is to 変更・取消の入口と連絡条件を明確にする. Extra choices can look helpful but often increase decision load. Move secondary information later and keep the immediate action visible.

## Plan the exception path

A normal path is not enough. When 期限後の相談が来た場合, define where human judgment starts, who owns the response, and how the customer can leave the automated path without getting stuck.

## Measure operational friction

Watch 変更方法を探す往復. Repeated questions, handoffs, and pauses often reveal more than a feature checklist. Improve one recurring friction point at a time.

## Keep product claims separate from workflow design

Confirm product-specific capabilities in current official documentation before implementation. This reserve article deliberately focuses on workflow principles and does not assume an undocumented product function.

## Keep the human work human

Use automation for predictable steps and preserve human attention for exceptions, reassurance, and judgment. A good workflow reduces unnecessary repetition without making the experience feel closed or mechanical.
