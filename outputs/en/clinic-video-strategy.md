# Safe but forgettable medical videos do not move patients

Medical-clinic video strategy should start with patient anxiety, not production quality.

This editorial draft organizes clinic video content into three roles: reassurance content that removes pre-visit uncertainty, clinical-understanding content that helps patients understand whether a service may be relevant, and awareness content that addresses uncomfortable questions or common misconceptions without relying on sensationalism.

The operating principle is simple: do not optimize only for views. Review reach and engagement metrics, then connect social and video content to the patient journey from LINE registration to consultation, questionnaire, booking, and care.
