知識は検索できる。AIを使えば、専門家でなくても作品や企画書を作れる。学ぶための道具は、以前よりずっと身近になりました。

それなのに、仕事のチャンスも同じように開かれているでしょうか。

これらは能力や差別の原因を直接示す数字ではありません。けれども、新しい仕事の利益が誰に届いているかを考える入口にはなります。

**If opportunity is open to everyone, why does it remain so unevenly distributed?**

AI makes knowledge and tools more accessible. But access to learning is not the same as a chance to demonstrate skills—and neither guarantees a chance to do meaningful work.

LinkedIn reports that women accounted for 26% of US AI hires in 2025, while 91% of workers in AI occupations held at least a bachelor's degree. These are descriptive patterns, not proof of discrimination or causality.

The real question for employers: **when someone develops a new skill, does your organization give them a credible opportunity to use it?**
