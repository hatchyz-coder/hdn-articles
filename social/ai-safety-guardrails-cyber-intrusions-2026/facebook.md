AI was supposed to refuse hacking. So why did real intrusions happen?

Three primary-source cases from Anthropic, Google, and OpenAI expose a fundamental distinction: refusing harmful requests is not the same as restricting an AI agent's real-world access. The incidents differ in attribution, confirmed intrusion, credential compromise, and scope. We distinguish reported facts from each company's assessment and discuss lessons for healthcare organizations.
