AI guardrails are not access controls.

Anthropic's AI-led espionage report, Google's rapid credential-harvesting findings, and OpenAI's research-agent incident each reveal a different risk. Attempted targets are not all confirmed victims; credentials compromised are not automatically evidence of every downstream leak; and experimental agents are not consumer ChatGPT.

Our analysis examines autonomy, tool permissions, vendor accountability, and concrete healthcare safeguards.
