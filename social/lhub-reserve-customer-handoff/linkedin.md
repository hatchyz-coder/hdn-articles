運用改善で重要なのは、機能数ではなく「どの摩擦を消すか」を決めることです。定型作業は仕組みに寄せ、判断が必要な対応は人に残します。

Small operational friction adds up. The useful question is not how many features a tool has, but which repeated interruption it can remove. Start with one workflow, keep exceptions human, and improve it from real questions that come back from staff and customers.
